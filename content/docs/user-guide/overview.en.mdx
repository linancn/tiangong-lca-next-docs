This section explains the platform interface and operations; it does not set LCA methodology. First use
the [documentation task page](/en/docs/) to choose **Assess a product’s environmental impacts** or
**Organise and publish LCA data**. Then browse this guide by getting started, data and modelling,
analysis and review, or collaboration and administration.

## Who should read this?

- **Data authors**: Build or maintain processes, models, and flows with consistent references.  
- **Quality reviewers**: Define review standards and monitor submission progress.  
- **Team collaborators**: Share datasets, manage permissions, and coordinate updates.  
- **Analysts & consumers**: Discover, copy, and analyse existing datasets.

## Recommended reading order

1. **Learn the interface**
   Start with the [Key Functions Overview](/en/docs/user-guide/key-functions-introduction/) to understand the top bar, avatar menu, list controls, and collaboration tools.
2. **Understand global import/export flows**
   Read [TIDAS ZIP Import, Export, and Task Center](/en/docs/user-guide/tidas-zip-workflows/) before handling package exchange or background jobs.
3. **Understand data modules**
   Read the [Data Platform Overview](/en/docs/user-guide/data/) to see how Open Data, My Data, Commercial Data, and Team Data fit together.
4. **Create & maintain data**
   Follow [Create My Data](/en/docs/user-guide/create-my-data/), [Data Use](/en/docs/user-guide/data-use/), and [Search](/en/docs/user-guide/search/) to go from raw references to reusable datasets.
5. **Validate & publish**
   Review [Data Review](/en/docs/user-guide/data-review/) for role-specific workspaces, workflows, and status tracking.
6. **Collaborate & understand permissions**
   Consult [Team Functions](/en/docs/user-guide/team-function/), [Permissions & Data Spaces](/en/docs/user-guide/permissions-and-data-scopes/), and [System Management Workspace](/en/docs/user-guide/system-management/).
7. **Analyse impacts**
   Explore [Process Analysis Workspace](/en/docs/user-guide/process-analysis/) and [LCIA Calculation & Results](/en/docs/user-guide/lcia/).

## Quick shortcuts

- Need to know which top-bar entry handles import, export, or background progress? Jump to [Key Functions Overview](/en/docs/user-guide/key-functions-introduction/) or [TIDAS ZIP Import, Export, and Task Center](/en/docs/user-guide/tidas-zip-workflows/).
- Creating a new dataset? Follow the “Prepare → Build datasets → Data validation” stages in [Create My Data](/en/docs/user-guide/create-my-data/).  
- Facing a review blocker? Check the role-specific guidance and FAQ in [Data Review](/en/docs/user-guide/data-review/).
- Need a fast permission comparison? Go straight to [Permissions & Data Spaces](/en/docs/user-guide/permissions-and-data-scopes/).
